# Rezdy

> Rezdy is online booking software and a channel manager for tour, activity and attraction operators, with its strongest presence in Australia and New Zealand. Operators use it to take direct bookings on their own website, manage availability and payments in one place, and sell through a large network of resellers and online travel agents (OTAs). Rezdy is part of Expedition, alongside Checkfront and Regiondo.

Key facts:
- Built for tours, activities, attractions, cruises and charters, rentals, shuttles and events.
- Includes a booking system, a channel manager connected to resellers and OTAs, payments (RezdyPay), automated guest communications and reporting.
- Pricing: three monthly plans plus a fee per online booking, which the operator can absorb or pass on to guests. Unlimited bookings, users and products on every plan, no lock-in contracts, and a 21-day free trial with no credit card required. See the pricing page for current rates.
